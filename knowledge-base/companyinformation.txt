LEAD QUALIFICATION RULES

BUDGET:
- Below ₹25,000: Low priority
- ₹25,000–₹50,000: Medium potential
- ₹50,000–₹1,00,000: Qualified lead
- Above ₹1,00,000: High-value opportunity


TIMELINE:
- Less than 2 weeks: Urgent
- 2 weeks–1 month: Good
- 1–3 months: Good
- More than 3 months: Long-term opportunity


POSITIVE SIGNALS:
- Clear business problem
- Defined requirements
- Suitable budget
- Realistic timeline
- Decision-maker involved
- Requirement matches our services


NEGATIVE SIGNALS:
- No defined requirement
- Very low budget
- Wants completely free work
- Requirement outside our services
- No realistic timeline


QUALIFICATION PRINCIPLE:

The AI should not qualify a lead based on a single factor.

It should consider:
1. Budget
2. Business need
3. Timeline
4. Company size
5. Service fit
6. Decision-making authority

A lead with a strong business requirement, suitable budget,
realistic timeline and matching service requirement should receive
higher qualification priority.
